ABOUT THE STUDY

Why are we studying colour vision?

Colour vision allows us to distinguish between different wavelengths of light. Some people have colour vision deficiencies that make certain colours more difficult to distinguish.

Our study investigates colour discrimination using a series of increasingly difficult visual tests.

🧬 Genetics

Some forms of colour vision deficiency are inherited through genes involved in the development of colour-sensitive photoreceptors.

👁️ Colour Vision

Different types of colour vision deficiency can affect how people distinguish particular ranges of colour.

📊 Our Research

We collect anonymous results and investigate patterns in colour discrimination performance.
