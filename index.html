THE STUDY

What are we investigating?

Colour vision depends on the way light is detected and processed by the visual system. This study explores how accurately people can distinguish between colours that are deliberately made similar.

01

Colour discrimination

Participants complete a series of visual tasks where they identify subtle differences between colours.

02

Human variation

Results can be compared between participants to investigate patterns in colour-discrimination performance.

03

Three colour axes

The experiment contains stimuli designed around three colour-confusion axes associated with different cone responses.

RESEARCH DESIGN

Designed as an experiment.

The test does not simply ask whether someone can identify colours. Different visual tasks are mixed throughout the experiment so that participants cannot predict which type of colour difference will appear next.
